# 🎮 The Journey

> An original open-world lifestyle and action game inspired by Ghanaian life, exploration, vehicles, and technology.

## 🌍 About the Game

**The Journey** is an ambitious open-world game project following a young university student as he navigates education, technology, exploration, transportation, opportunities, and everyday life in a Ghana-inspired world.

The player begins with very little and gradually builds their life through exploration, decisions, skills, technology, vehicles, and missions.

## 🎯 Core Gameplay

- 🌍 Open-world exploration
- 🚗 Vehicles and transportation
- 💻 Technology and engineering
- 🎯 Story and missions
- 🏫 University life
- 💰 Money and progression
- 🏠 Lifestyle and personal development
- 🗺️ Discoverable locations
- 👤 Character progression

## 🌆 The World

The game will feature an original Ghana-inspired environment containing:

- 🏫 University areas
- 🏘️ Residential neighborhoods
- 🏪 Shops and businesses
- 🛣️ Roads and highways
- 🌳 Rural and natural areas
- 🔧 Workshops
- 💻 Technology spaces
- 🚗 Vehicle-related locations

The world will grow gradually as development continues.

The goal is not to copy existing open-world games.

**The Journey** aims to create its own identity by combining:

> **Ghanaian-inspired life + Exploration + Vehicles + Technology + Action**
